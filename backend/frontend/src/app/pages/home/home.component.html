Our Mission

Government Data Buddy democratizes access to congressional information, making it easier for citizens, journalists, researchers, and policymakers to understand and engage with the legislative process. We provide a user-friendly interface to the official Congress.gov API, transforming complex government data into accessible, actionable insights.
