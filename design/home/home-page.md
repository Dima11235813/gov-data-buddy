## **Government Data Buddy App Home Page Design Prompt**

### **Overview**

We're enhancing the "Government Data Buddy" app's homepage to reflect its full range of capabilities, leveraging the entire Congress.gov API. The goal is to create a dynamic, user-friendly, and informative home page that highlights all the data points we can access. We need to go beyond just bills and committees and showcase the breadth of our data, including laws, members, amendments, and more.

### **Core Identity**

* **App Name:** Government Data Buddy 🏛️
* **Tagline:** Your comprehensive portal for U.S. Congressional data.
* **Tone:** Professional, reliable, and approachable. The design should feel clean, modern, and trustworthy.

### **Key Sections and Content**

Please revise the existing homepage design to integrate the following new and expanded sections. Maintain the "enterprise-grade" and "user-friendly" messaging.

#### **1. Hero Section**

* **Headline:** "Government Data, Made Simple."
* **Sub-headline:** "Access a comprehensive database of U.S. Congressional information with an intuitive interface, enterprise-grade architecture, and intelligent caching."
* **Call-to-Action Buttons:**
    * "Start Your Search" (primary)
    * "See All Data Sources" (secondary)

#### **2. Data Sources Overview**

Replace the old "Bills" and "Committees" sections with a more comprehensive grid or card-based layout. Each card should feature an icon, a clear title, and a brief, compelling description. The descriptions should hint at the rich data available for each category.

* **Bills:** "Track every piece of legislation with full text, summaries, and real-time status updates."
* **Laws:** "Find and explore enacted Public and Private Laws, including their legislative history."
* **Members of Congress:** "Get detailed biographies, sponsored and co-sponsored legislation, and contact information for every member."
* **Committees:** "Explore committee structures, member rosters, meeting schedules, and associated reports."
* **Amendments:** "Review proposed changes to legislation, including sponsors and voting records."
* **Congressional Record:** "Access the official record of daily floor debates and proceedings, both in daily and bound formats."
* **House & Senate Communications:** "Browse formal communications from the Executive Branch and other government agencies to Congress."
* **Nominations:** "Monitor Presidential nominations, their status, and the committees reviewing them."
* **CRS Reports:** "Access in-depth, non-partisan research and analysis from the Congressional Research Service."
* **Treaties:** "Review detailed information on U.S. treaties, their related documents, and committee actions."

#### **3. Expanded Features Section**

This section should emphasize the technology and user experience. Use the existing messaging but expand on what it means with our new data sources.

* **Intelligent Search:** "Our powerful search lets you filter and sort across all data types, from bills and laws to members and committee prints."
* **Performance & Reliability:** "Built with a modern architecture, including PostgreSQL and Redis, for high-speed data delivery and a flawless user experience."
* **Up-to-Date Information:** "Stay current with real-time updates synchronized directly from the Congress.gov API."

#### **4. Statistics & Metrics**

Keep this section prominent, updating the numbers to reflect the full data scope. We need new metrics that are more general.

* **1000s** of Bills & Laws
* **500+** Members of Congress
* **90%** Faster API Calls
* **99.9%** Uptime

#### **5. Footer**

Update the footer with links to all the new data categories.

* **Quick Links:** Bills, Laws, Members, Committees, Amendments, Nominations, etc.
* **Legal:** Acknowledge the use of Congress.gov API data.

### **Design Considerations**

* Use a consistent visual style and color palette.
* Incorporate icons for each data source to make them scannable and visually distinct.
* Ensure the design is responsive and looks great on desktop and mobile.
* Use a U.S. Capitol building or similar governmental landmark as a subtle background element to reinforce the theme, as you have. Maybe combine it with a modern, digital overlay or data visualization pattern.

Let's make this app's homepage a powerful directory that truly showcases the comprehensive data we offer.
